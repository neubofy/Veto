# Veto

Veto is an advanced, completely free, privacy-first, and highly resilient remote management and anti-theft application.

It provides you with the ultimate toolkit to locate, control, and recover your lost or stolen device. Unlike traditional third-party tracking apps, Veto does not rely on continuous background workers—it remains entirely dormant until a command is received or the phone is stolen, eliminating battery drain and performance issues.

We don't aim to replace Google's Find My Device network; instead, Veto is designed to work *with* it. We provide everything that is missing in Google Find My Device, prioritizing actions and commands over continuous live tracking when offline.

## Features
- **Advanced Theft Mode:** Uniquely designed for maximum deterrence and recovery. When triggered (even offline via SMS), it automatically bypasses Do Not Disturb to blare a max-volume siren, activates a mesh-network-style Radar UI on the device, toggles the flashlight, enables Bluetooth (to aid Google Find My Device localization), and begins silently capturing background photos and audio to secure your data.
- **Offline Mesh & SMS Control:** Remotely trigger actions without an internet connection using secure, offline SMS commands. Power users can even integrate it with other offline mesh network-based messaging apps to communicate when completely offline.
- **Locate without GPS:** The location command operates effectively even if the GPS toggle is turned off on the device.
- **Unstoppable Ring:** The ring command overrides the volume down button to ensure a max-volume alert (a siren ringtone is recommended).
- **Self-Hosted Ready:** Easily spin up your own Vercel dashboard and Firebase instance to retain 100% control over your data.
